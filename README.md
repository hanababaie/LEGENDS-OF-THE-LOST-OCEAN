# Legends of the Lost Ocean 🏝️🐚  

**Legends of the Lost Ocean** is a **2D fantasy adventure game** built with **Unity**.  
Play as **Jack** and **Will**, two treasure pirates who embark on an epic journey through mysterious islands, ships with mysterious enemies, and dangerous seas to claim the **ultimate hidden treasure**.  

---

## 🎮 Game Features
- **Adventure Gameplay** – Explore islands, ships, and oceans full of secrets.  
- **Local Co-op** – Play side by side with a friend on the same screen and same system.  
- **Online Co-op** – Join forces with a friend remotely.  
- **3 Stages**:
  1. Survive a mysterious island filled with hostile sea creatures.  
  2. Conquer a ship by defeating its crew and enemies.  
  3. Face the **giant crab boss** in the lost ocean to reach the final treasure.  
- **Fantasy Art Style** – A colorful, 2D cartoon world.  

---

## 🕹️ Controls
- **Player 1 (Jack)**:  
  - Movement: `Arrow Keys`  
  - Jump: `Space`
  - Attack: `Ctrl`
    

- **Player 2 (Will)**:  
  - Movement: `WASD`  
  - Jump: `Shift`
  - Attack: `Tab`

*(Controls can be customized in Unity Input Settings.)*
